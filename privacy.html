Privacy policy

Tuner for YouTube - last updated August 2026

The short version

Tuner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data. Everything the extension stores (your settings, notes, playlist groups, and background image) stays in your browser's local extension storage on your own device.

What the extension stores locally

  • Your feature toggles and appearance settings
  • Notes you write on videos, keyed by video ID
  • Playlist groups and which videos you assigned to them
  • A background image, if you upload one

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it. Some settings use Chrome's built-in sync storage, which Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers; that syncing is handled entirely by Google Chrome, not by Tuner.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind
  • No reading of your watch history, subscriptions, or account data
  • No servers of our own: the extension makes no network requests to us
  • No selling or sharing of any information with anyone

Payments

The optional Pro upgrade is handled by ExtensionPay, with payment processing by Stripe. If you choose to pay, your payment details are collected and processed by Stripe on their pages, subject to their privacy policies. Tuner itself never sees your payment information; it only learns whether the upgrade is active.

Permissions

The extension requests access to youtube.com so it can modify YouTube's appearance and behavior, storage so it can save your settings and notes on your device, and extensionpay.com so the payment page can confirm a purchase.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing's support section.

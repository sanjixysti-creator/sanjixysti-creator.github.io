Safety first

  • Never mix SH with acids or ammonia. That includes some rust removers and wheel cleaners. The mix gives off toxic gas.
  • Protect yourself. Wear splash goggles, chemical resistant gloves and clothing that covers your skin. Mix outdoors or with plenty of fresh air.
  • Use vented containers. SH slowly gives off gas, so a tightly sealed container can build pressure.
  • Read the label and the safety data sheet for your SH and your surfactant. This calculator only does the math.

Common questions

How much sodium hypochlorite goes in the tank?

Divide the strength you want by the strength of your SH. That is the share of the tank that is SH. For example, a 3% mix from 12.5% SH is 24% SH, so a 50 gallon tank takes 12 gallons of SH. The rest is water and surfactant.

Does surfactant change the SH amount?

No. Surfactant is a small share of the tank, so it only trims the water. The SH amount depends on the batch size, the strength you want and the strength of your SH.

Why does the strength printed on my SH matter?

Liquid SH is sold at different strengths. Pool chlorine is commonly 10% to 12.5%, and household bleach is weaker. The same tank needs more of a weaker product, so use the number on your label.

Does SH lose strength over time?

Yes. Liquid SH breaks down as it ages, and faster in heat and sunlight. If yours has been sitting, test it or enter a lower strength than the label says.

What strength should I mix for a house or a roof?

That depends on the surface and on how much growth is on it, so follow your chemical supplier's guidance or the label of your product. Once you have the number, Rinse Mix works out the amounts.

How the math worksTap to open

A mix's strength is the SH volume times its strength, divided by the total volume. So the share of the tank that has to be SH is the strength you want divided by the strength of your SH.

Surfactant is its dose times the batch size, and water is whatever is left. This assumes the liquids add up to the batch volume, which is close enough for these mixes.

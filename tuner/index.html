YouTube, but the way you want it.

Tuner is a Chrome extension that puts you in charge of YouTube: strip out the noise, keep notes on what you watch, sort giant playlists into tidy groups, and reskin the whole site to match your taste.

Add to Chrome Chrome Web Store listing in review

What it does

Hide Shorts and Playables

Removes Shorts and the games shelf from your feed, sidebar, search, and suggestions. One toggle each.

Playlists open as lists

Clicking a playlist takes you to the playlist page instead of autoplaying the first video.

Notes on any video

A notes panel next to the player that saves as you type and exports to a file. Stored on your device.

Sub playlists PRO

Group the videos inside any playlist into your own categories, or let Auto group sort them by topic in one click.

Themes PRO

Your own accent color, font, and full background image with a readability dim, applied across all of YouTube.

Private by design

Everything lives in your browser. No accounts, no tracking, no data leaves your machine.

How to play

  1. Press and drag to spray. On a touch screen the spray lands a little above your finger so you can see it. With a keyboard, move the nozzle with the arrow keys or WASD and hold Space to spray.
  2. Dirt comes in layers. Dust comes off with any tip, thick crust needs a narrow, strong tip or soap first. When nothing comes off you will see sparks and a hint.
  3. Pick your tip. Red is tiny and brutal, then yellow, green and white, which is wide and gentle. Red can scuff wood and siding if you linger.
  4. Keep spraying without lifting to build your flow bonus. A job finishes at 96 percent clean. Scrub the tough spots by hand for the cleanliness bonus.
  5. Spend cash on rig upgrades, gear and crew. Crew earns while you are away. A Franchise restarts the business for a permanent income boost.

About

Grime Time is a free pressure washing game for your browser. Blast layers of grime off driveways, decks, cars and more, get paid, upgrade your rig and hire a crew. Finish a job, then slide between the before and after and share the picture. It is made by Xysti Software, which also builds free calculators for real pressure washing businesses.

Grime Time runs in your browser, with no ads, no sign up and no tracking.

Your progress saves on this device only. Clearing your browser data erases it, so use Export save in Settings to keep a copy or to move to another device.
